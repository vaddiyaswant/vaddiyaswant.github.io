Yaswant Vaddi

Experimental physicist working across nanophotonics, nonlinear optics, metamaterials and plasmonics.

What I enjoy most is the full cycle of a photonic device: designing and simulating it, fabricating it in the cleanroom, and testing it in the lab. Working through every stage has taught me how design choices play out in fabrication and measurement, and how to move a device smoothly from one step to the next.

My current focus is strong light–matter coupling between quantum emitters and plasmonic nanoantennas, for a new generation of quantum sensors. I'm also extending my work into silicon photonics, designing photonic integrated circuits through UBC's silicon photonics design–fabricate–test course.
